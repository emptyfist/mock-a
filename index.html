What is AllWeb3?

AllWeb3 is a Web3 marketing platform that connects projects with creators and communities for performance-based campaigns. The platform focuses on transparent tracking and verifiable results so projects can run campaigns with clearer accountability, and creators can earn based on validated performance.

How does “Pay Only for Verified Performance” work?

Campaign results are verified onchain, and engagement is evaluated with AI scoring. Brands pay for validated performance, with transparent tracking of campaign results.

How do projects launch a campaign?

Choose “I'm a Brand” and select “Launch Campaign” to get started. Set your campaign goals, define the rewards, and connect with creators who match your project.

How do creators earn rewards on AllWeb3?

Browse campaigns that match your audience, create and share content, and earn rewards based on verified contributions.

What is SPC (Success Proof Certificate)?

An SPC is a Success Proof Certificate. It represents verified results and helps creators build a record of their campaign performance.
